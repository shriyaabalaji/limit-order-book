what i did -
implemented my own order book (very basic implementation) using a flat array indexed by price tick, an intrusive doubly linked list (FIFO queue) per price level, and a preallocated node pool with a free list
benchmarked against a few implementations
map+list 68 ns vs my book 4 ns per insert (16×)
my implementation faster, but assumes a dense bounded price range

larger takeaways -
what goes into determining deisgn -> efficiency of adding, filling, and cancelling orders
this is determined by what data structures you use
best implementation will change based on what financial asset because price range/memory will differ
-> aka crypto has a lot larger, sparse price range vs typical stocks are bounded and more dense
oh yeah you can consider caching as well for how long it'll take, not just big O
