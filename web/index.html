How matching works
  • Price priority: a buy trades with the lowest ask first; a sell with the highest bid.
  • Time priority: at the same price, the oldest order fills first (FIFO).
  • Trade price is the resting order's price, not yours.
  • Partial fills: whatever doesn't trade rests in the book at your limit.
  • Self-trade prevention: if your order would hit your own resting order, matching stops and the rest of your order is cancelled.
