How this perp works
  • Mark price = midpoint of the best bid and ask. It values every position.
  • Initial margin 10%: to open, your equity must cover 10% of the position, counting your resting orders as if they filled (so max 10x).
  • Maintenance margin 5%: if equity falls below 5% of the position, you're liquidated: your orders are cancelled and the whole position is market-sold into the book.
  • Cascades: a liquidation can push the mark down far enough to liquidate the next trader.
  • Bad debt: if the book is too thin and a position closes below zero equity, the winners were paid money the loser didn't have.
  • Funding: every interval, longs pay shorts (mark − spot) per contract, capped at 0.5%. Shorts pay when the perp is below spot. It pulls the perp toward spot.

Try: go ~9x long (about 880 contracts), then hit Spot −6%.
